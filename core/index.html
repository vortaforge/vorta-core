Datapacks & resource packs

The datapack programme is retired and disabled.
Every datapack, datapack generator, linter and test harness was archived on 2026-11-05 and hard-disarmed (archived/archive.json). No datapack is built, published or supported any more, and new ones are blocked before they land.

Reason: no type safety, no security model, no debugging, no warnings, and a low review score — bad enough that it would have been better not to ship it at all. The maintainer also hit real problems as a plain player using a datapack with no client mod installed.

The archived packs stay readable in the repository under archived/packs/ for reference only. Do not deploy them.
