Which ones you actually need

me_core_module is the only required pack. Everything else is optional and independent: installing me_cb_module without me_events_module is a valid install, and so is the reverse.

The two that are easiest to assume are required, and are not:

PackIf you leave it out
me_events_module Core’s deferred queue keeps filling and nothing drains it. Hooks that fire engine events still fire their own handlers; the bus is what you lose.
me_text_moduleNothing. Its five functions are not called from anywhere in the engine.
Why math, rng, log, string, color and validate are not modules. They were candidates. Each is called by the core at boot or on tick, so making them modules would make the core depend on a module — the one thing the layout forbids. A pack the engine cannot boot without is part of the engine. They live in me_core_module.
